Who we are

Local action.
Lasting possibility.

Social Edify in Jhenaidah Almorny (SEJA) is a non-political, voluntary, non-profit organization established in 1999.

Guided by its constitution, SEJA's objectives include education, health awareness, employment and skills development, women's employment, support for vulnerable groups, environmental awareness, and prevention of substance abuse among adolescents and young people.

SEJA works from Jhenaidah, Bangladesh, and seeks to support positive community participation and development.
